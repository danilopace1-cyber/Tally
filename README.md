Tally — a teleprompter that follows your voice

https://danilopace1-cyber.github.io/Telly/

Open the link on your phone. Optional but worth it: Share → Add to Home Screen, so it opens fullscreen like an app.
Tap "Script", paste in what you want to read, then tap "Use this".
Tap "Run" to start scrolling. The slider sets the speed.
Tap "Voice" to have it follow you instead — allow the microphone when it asks. Now it moves as you speak, waits when you pause, and speeds up or slows down to match you.
Tap any word to jump the cue there if you lose your place. "Top" goes back to the start.

Read at the red line near the top — that's where your eyes stay closest to the camera lens.

Use Chrome or Safari. "Mirror" flips the text if you're using a prompter with a glass reflector.
