Run scrolls at the WPM you set, whatever else is happening. Voice can be on at the same time: it snaps the cue to the words you've actually said and nudges the speed toward how fast you're talking, so a pause or an ad-lib doesn't leave you chasing the text. If the mic drops out, the scroll just carries on at your set speed.

Prev restarts the sentence you're on — press it again to step back another. Handy when someone asks you to take it from the line before. You can also scroll with your finger and the cue adopts wherever you stop, or tap any single word to move it there. Space runs and pauses, V toggles voice, ← → move a sentence, ↑ ↓ nudge a line, Esc opens this panel.
